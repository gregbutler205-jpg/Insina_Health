You control your data.

Insina Health is non-custodial: we never hold your record. Here is what that means in practice.

Encrypted on your device
Your record is encrypted at rest with a password and a recovery key. On your phone, a PIN protects access.
Stored in your Google Drive
Sync uses your own Google account. Your record lives in your Drive, under your credentials. We never store it.
Identity-minimized AI
AI requests carry a pseudonymous ID. Your name, DOB, and contact details are never sent.
Export or delete anytime
Take your complete record with you, disconnect storage, or erase everything, whenever you choose.

Built from lived experience

Insina Health was built by a liver transplant recipient who needed one place to hold the medications, labs, records, and appointments spread across nine physicians and seven patient portals.
